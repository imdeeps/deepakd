Common questions

Everything you need to know before we start.

How much does a typical project cost?+

Our projects range from ₹80K for focused strategy engagements to ₹8L+ for full brand-to-website builds. We agree a fixed cost upfront — no surprise invoices. Get in touch and we'll give you a clear scope and price within 48 hours.

How long does a project typically take?+

A website project runs 10–14 weeks. A full brand-into-website-and-content engagement takes 3–6 months. We'll give you a clear milestone timeline from day one, and we respect your deadlines without cutting corners on quality.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?+

Absolutely. We're remote-first and have worked with clients across India and internationally. Our process is built for async collaboration — you'll always know where things stand without needing to be in the same room.

What makes DG Brandfluencer different from other agencies?+

We put strategy before execution — always. We won't start designing until we understand your positioning. Most agencies post first and think later. We think first, and then make every post count.

What does ongoing support look like after launch?+

We offer flexible retainer options covering content, ads, SEO and strategic oversight. Think of us as a fractional marketing team — senior thinking without the full-time overhead. Most clients stay with us 12+ months after their initial project.
