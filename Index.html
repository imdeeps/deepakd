How we think

Straight answers to the questions founders ask us.

On content strategy

"Why does my content get likes but zero enquiries?"

Because likes are a vanity metric and enquiries are a business metric. Content that entertains gets liked. Content that educates, builds trust and has a clear next step gets you clients. The fix isn't posting more. It's posting with intent.

On brand positioning

"I have a great product. Why isn't it selling online?"

Great products don't sell themselves — clear positioning does. If your audience can't explain what you do and why it's different in one sentence, you have a positioning problem, not a product problem.

On digital advertising

"I ran ads and wasted money. Are Meta ads even worth it?"

Almost always, the problem isn't the platform — it's what the ad points at. Sending paid traffic to a weak website or an unclear offer is pouring water into a leaking bucket. Fix the bucket first.
